# Attribution

BookCraft AI is an independent derivative project inspired by and adapted from concepts and implementation patterns in [`alchaincyf/huashu-report`](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-report), which is licensed under the MIT License.

The upstream project focuses on institution-grade research reports. BookCraft AI changes the product domain to long-form book production, especially Chinese memoirs, oral histories, biographies, family books, and image-rich manuscripts.

Upstream concepts intentionally carried forward include:

- HTML/CSS to PDF production workflow
- automatic table-of-contents page-number convergence
- post-render mechanical QA
- reusable visual-system assets
- long-document production discipline

BookCraft-specific work includes book semantics, front matter, chapter openers, mirrored margins, recto/verso behavior, image/caption rules, footnote handling, memoir fidelity rules, print-oriented QA, and editable-output workflows.
